<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5f/Lorenzo_Ruiz.jpg" alt="Lorenzo Ruiz"><div class="card-content"><h2>1. St. Lorenzo Ruiz</h2><span class="tag">First Filipino Saint - Martyr 1637</span><p>The first Filipino saint. From Binondo, Manila. He was martyred in Nagasaki, Japan for refusing to deny his Catholic faith. His last words: "If I had a thousand lives, I would offer them all to God."</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7a/San_Pedro_Calungsod.jpg" alt="Pedro Calungsod"><div class="card-content"><h2>2. St. Pedro Calungsod</h2><span class="tag brown">Second Filipino Saint - Martyr 1672</span><p>A young catechist from Cebu, only 14 when he joined the mission to Guam. He was killed while baptizing natives with Fr. San Vitores.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9b/Takayama_Ukon.jpg" alt="Takayama Ukon"><div class="card-content"><h2>3. Blessed Justo Takayama Ukon</h2><span class="tag">Blessed - Samurai of Christ</span><p>A Japanese Samurai Lord who gave up wealth and honor for his Catholic faith. Exiled to Manila where he died in 1615.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0e/Diego_Luis_de_San_Vitores.jpg" alt="San Vitores"><div class="card-content"><h2>4. Blessed Diego Luis de San Vitores</h2><span class="tag brown">Blessed - Missionary</span><p>A Jesuit missionary who brought Christianity to Guam and the Mariana Islands. Martyred with Pedro Calungsod.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3a/Jose_Maria_de_Manila.jpg" alt="Jose Maria"><div class="card-content"><h2>5. Blessed Jose Maria de Manila</h2><span class="tag">Blessed - Martyr of WWII 1945</span><p>A Capuchin priest killed in Intramuros during World War II. He chose to stay with the people until death.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f0/Mother_Ignacia.jpg" alt="Ignacia"><div class="card-content"><h2>6. Ven. Ignacia del Espiritu Santo</h2><span class="tag brown">Venerable - First Filipina Nun</span><p>Founded the Congregation of the Religious of the Virgin Mary (RVM) in 1684. A simple Filipina who served God through service.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2e/Teofilo_Camomot.jpg" alt="Camomot"><div class="card-content"><h2>7. Ven. Teofilo Camomot</h2><span class="tag">Venerable - Bishop of Cebu</span><p>Bishop from Cebu known for simplicity and love for the poor. Many miracles are reported at his tomb in Carcar, Cebu.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8a/Maria_Beatriz_Arroyo.jpg" alt="Arroyo"><div class="card-content"><h2>8. Ven. Maria Beatriz Arroyo</h2><span class="tag brown">Venerable - Foundress</span><p>Founded the Dominican Sisters of the Most Holy Rosary in Iloilo. She cared for orphans and the sick.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a7/Richard_Fernando.jpg" alt="Richard Fernando"><div class="card-content"><h2>9. Servant of God Richard Fernando</h2><span class="tag">Martyr - Age 26</span><p>A Jesuit brother who served in Cambodia. In 1996, he hugged a man with a grenade to save his students, sacrificing his own life.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5e/Rhoel_Gallardo.jpg" alt="Gallardo"><div class="card-content"><h2>10. Servant of God Fr. Rhoel Gallardo</h2><span class="tag brown">Martyr - Claretian 2000</span><p>A Claretian priest martyred by Abu Sayyaf in Basilan in 2000. He refused to leave his parish even when it was dangerous.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1d/Alfredo_Obviar.jpg" alt="Obviar"><div class="card-content"><h2>11. Servant of God Alfredo Obviar</h2><span class="tag">Bishop - Founder</span><p>First Bishop of Lucena. Founded the Missionary Catechists of St. Therese to teach in remote barrios.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c2/Alfredo_Versoza.jpg" alt="Verzosa"><div class="card-content"><h2>12. Servant of God Alfredo Verzosa</h2><span class="tag brown">Bishop - Founder</span><p>Bishop of Lipa. Founded a congregation to teach catechism in poor provinces.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9e/Francisca_del_Espiritu_Santo.jpg" alt="Francisca"><div class="card-content"><h2>13. Servant of God Francisca del Espiritu Santo</h2><span class="tag">First Filipina Dominican</span><p>The first Filipina Dominican nun. Founded the Beaterio de Santa Catalina in 1696.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6d/Isabel_Larra%C3%B1aga.jpg" alt="Isabel"><div class="card-content"><h2>14. Servant of God Isabel Larrañaga Ramirez</h2><span class="tag brown">Foundress</span><p>Known as Mother Isabel. Founded schools for orphans and cared for the sick in the Philippines.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7c/Consuelo_Barcelo.jpg" alt="Barcelo"><div class="card-content"><h2>15. Servant of God Consuelo Barcelo</h2><span class="tag">Augustinian Sister</span><p>Founded the Augustinian Sisters in the Philippines and built schools and hospitals.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d3/George_Willmann.jpg" alt="Willmann"><div class="card-content"><h2>16. Servant of God George Willmann</h2><span class="tag brown">Father of Knights of Columbus PH</span><p>American Jesuit who brought Knights of Columbus to the Philippines to help the poor and the Church.</p></div></div>

<div class="card"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4a/Joseph_Aveni.jpg" alt="Aveni"><div class="card-content"><h2>17. Servant of God Joseph Aveni</h2><span class="tag">Rogationist Priest</span><p>Italian priest who served the poor in Cavite, especially street children.</p></div></div>
