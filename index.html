LIVES FOR GOD

17 Filipino Saints & Servants - Be blessings

1. St. Lorenzo Ruiz

Martyr 1637

First Filipino Saint from Binondo, Manila. Martyred in Nagasaki, Japan.

2. St. Pedro Calungsod

Martyr 1672

Young catechist from Cebu, 14 yrs old. Martyred in Guam while baptizing.

3. Blessed Justo Takayama Ukon

Samurai

Japanese Samurai Lord who left wealth for faith. Died in Manila 1615.

4. Blessed Diego Luis de San Vitores

Missionary

Jesuit who brought Christianity to Guam.

5. Blessed Jose Maria de Manila

Martyr WWII

Capuchin priest killed in Intramuros 1945.

6. Ven. Ignacia del Espiritu Santo

First Filipina Nun

Founded RVM Sisters in 1684. Served Filipino women.

7. Ven. Teofilo Camomot

Bishop Cebu

Bishop of Cebu, known for miracles at Carcar tomb.

8. Ven. Maria Beatriz Arroyo

Foundress Iloilo

Founded Dominican Sisters in Iloilo. Cared for orphans.

9. Richard Fernando

Martyr 26 yrs

Jesuit in Cambodia. Hugged man with grenade to save students 1996.

10. Fr. Rhoel Gallardo

Martyr 2000 Basilan

Claretian priest killed by Abu Sayyaf.

11. Alfredo Obviar

Bishop Lucena

First Bishop of Lucena. Founded Missionary Catechists.

12. Alfredo Verzosa

Bishop of Lipa

Bishop who built schools to teach catechism.

13. Francisca del Espiritu Santo

Dominican 1696

First Filipina Dominican, founded Beaterio de Santa Catalina.

14. Isabel Larrañaga

Mother Isabel

Founded schools for orphans and sick children.

15. Consuelo Barcelo

Augustinian

Founded Augustinian Sisters, built schools and hospitals.

16. George Willmann

Jesuit USA

Brought Knights of Columbus to Philippines.

17. Joseph Aveni

Rogationist Cavite

Served poor in Cavite, helped street children.
