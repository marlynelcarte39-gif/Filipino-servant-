17 Filipino Saints & Servants - Blue & Brown Design

1. St. Lorenzo Ruiz

Martyr 1637

First Filipino Saint from Binondo. Martyred in Nagasaki. Said: If I had 1000 lives, I would give them all to God.

2. St. Pedro Calungsod

Martyr 1672

Young catechist from Cebu, 14 yrs old. Martyred in Guam while baptizing.

3. Blessed Justo Takayama Ukon

Samurai

Japanese Lord who left wealth for faith. Died in Manila 1615.

4. Blessed Diego Luis de San Vitores

Missionary

Jesuit who brought Christianity to Guam. Died with Pedro Calungsod.

5. Blessed Jose Maria de Manila

Martyr WWII

Capuchin priest killed in Intramuros 1945.

6. Ven. Ignacia del Espiritu Santo

First Filipina Nun

Founded RVM in 1684.

7. Ven. Teofilo Camomot

Bishop

Bishop of Cebu, known for miracles in Carcar.

8. Ven. Maria Beatriz Arroyo

Foundress

Founded Dominican Sisters in Iloilo.

9. Richard Fernando

Martyr 26

Jesuit in Cambodia. Hugged man with grenade to save students 1996.

10. Fr. Rhoel Gallardo

Martyr 2000

Claretian priest killed by Abu Sayyaf in Basilan.

11. Alfredo Obviar

Bishop

First Bishop of Lucena. Founded Missionary Catechists.

12. Alfredo Verzosa

Bishop of Lipa

Built schools to teach catechism.

13. Francisca del Espiritu Santo

Dominican

First Filipina Dominican, founded Beaterio 1696.

14. Isabel Larrañaga

Mother Isabel

Founded schools for orphans.

15. Consuelo Barcelo

Augustinian

Founded Augustinian Sisters.

16. George Willmann

Jesuit

Brought Knights of Columbus to PH.

17. Joseph Aveni

Rogationist

Served poor in Cavite, helped street children.
