Important: “Servant of God,” “Venerable,” “Blessed,” and “Saint” are not interchangeable titles. This website uses the status associated with each person's cause or canonization. A person being a Servant of God does not mean the Church has declared that person a saint.

The Path to Sainthood

In simplified form, the Catholic Church examines a person's life, virtues, martyrdom and—when required—miracles.

1

Servant of God

The cause is formally opened and the person's life and reputation for holiness are investigated.

2

Venerable

The Church recognizes heroic virtue, or the appropriate basis for advancing the cause.

3

Blessed

Beatification normally follows recognition of a miracle, or recognition of martyrdom under the Church's norms.

4

Saint

Canonization is the final declaration, normally requiring another recognized miracle after beatification.

About this project

“Lives for God” is a student-friendly Catholic heritage website designed to introduce readers to people associated with the Philippines and its Catholic mission. The information below is a concise starting point, not a substitute for official Church documents.
